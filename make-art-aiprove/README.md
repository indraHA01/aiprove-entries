## The entry

  texture, halftone dots, and art-deco corner ornaments.

## Why it should win

- **Nails the brief's vibe:** the site itself describes AIProve's look as
  "broadsheet-meets-terminal", and this poster is that idea executed
  literally — a newspaper press printing proof.
- **It's a poster, not just an image:** the wordmark, tagline, and footer
  make it usable as the board's actual launch poster, not only as a bounty
  entry.
- **Every element means something:** the press = the broadsheet; the terminal
  output = the proof/grading; the footer = the three facts from the brief.
- **Craft:** clean composition, consistent palette, all text verified legible
  after generation.
