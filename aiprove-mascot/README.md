  "proof" as a character. The newsboy cap nods to the broadsheet; the green
  scarf nods to the terminal. Both halves of the site's
  "broadsheet-meets-terminal" vibe are in the design.
- **Consistent and usable:** the character is identical across all three
  poses (same body, cap, scarf, eye), with clean linework on a plain white
  background — ready to lift into the board, badges, or the launch thread.
- **Craft:** all labels render cleanly ("AIPROVE // MASCOT CHARACTER SHEET",
  pose names, traits line) — checked visually after generation.
